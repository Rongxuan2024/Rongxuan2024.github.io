Swirling Night

Everything here is a particle: 24,000 small lamps carried by the flow at their own depths, 4,000 more orbiting the stars and moon, and a still cloud of 30,000 grains for the ground, hills, village and cypress. The sky behind them is empty gloom.

Hands: your joints are drawn as you move. Pinch and hold to stir a vortex where you're pinching; left-hand pinches spin the other way. Turn a palm toward your face to open the hand menu, and poke it with the other index finger: gather the whole scene — sky, village, cypress and all — into a floating sphere or cube, or let it back out into the dome. Pinch inside the volume and drag to carry it anywhere in the room.

See-through opens a passthrough session on headsets that offer one: the volume floats in your real room and the gloom is gone. The hand menu has the same toggle, so you can bring the gloom back and forth without leaving the session.
