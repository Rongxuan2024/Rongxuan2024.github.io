On your screen, move your pointer to stir the stars. Hold to gather them, then release. Drag with the right mouse button to look around; use W, A, S, D and Q / E to move.

In a compatible WebXR headset, enter immersive mode and enable hand tracking. Reach into the nearby stars, pinch your thumb and index finger to gather them, and release to let them drift. Controllers also work: point and hold the trigger.

Your head and physical movement guide the view in all six degrees of freedom. Stay within your headset’s boundary.
